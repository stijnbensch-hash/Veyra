# Streaming wordmarks

Bundled in `Shared/StreamingLogos.xcassets`, shared by tvOS, iOS and macOS. Retrieved 2026-10-03. Logos identify the service; no endorsement is implied. Rights in the marks remain with their respective owners.

Preparation: transparent, tightly bounded PNGs capped at 768 × 256 pixels; template rendering is white at rest and tinted on focus. Custom user artwork takes precedence. No full-resolution source images are loaded at runtime. NPO Start uses the original diamond lockup with contrasting letters cut out of its alpha mask. FXNow's opaque black provider background was removed to isolate the complete wordmark. The VRT MAX, Videoland and SkyShowtime SVG paths were taken from their official site headers. JustWatch TV uses the full JustWatch wordmark.
